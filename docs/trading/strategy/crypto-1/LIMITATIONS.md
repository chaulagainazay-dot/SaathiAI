# Limitations

- Historical archive publication timestamps cannot be reconstructed; bar values are
  conservatively available only after exclusive bar close.
- Binance may later publish a replacement archive. Such a checksum change is a new
  dataset revision and does not rewrite this qualification.
- Formal multiple-testing statistical correction is not asserted; 72 exact trials are
  reported as `MULTIPLE_TESTING_LIMITED`.
- Cost assumptions are conservative configured research values, not account-specific
  Binance fees.
- BTC mean reversion underperformed BTC buy-and-hold on TEST and lost money in TRAIN;
  the paper-candidate status depends on positive final OOS, both positive pre-TEST
  walk-forward segments, controlled drawdown, and cost/stress survival.
- No result is statistically significant, live-ready, leveraged, or authorized for
  execution.
- Durable RESEARCH-3 runtime persistence remains required before long-running
  PAPER/SHADOW operation. The exact qualification artifact is retained locally
  read-only, but the full restart-safe decision/signal/intent/outcome journal is not.
- Corporate-action and general reference-data PIT certification remain outside this
  crypto Spot scope. NEPSE costs remain unverified.
