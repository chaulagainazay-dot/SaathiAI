# M62.3 — Research Authority

Research agents may plan, read authorized sources + verified market data, extract
claims, compare evidence, generate hypotheses/scenarios, identify risks, challenge
theses, and produce draft artifacts — only through server-authorized workflows.

They may NOT: submit orders, create broker requests, approve/consume trading
approvals, bypass PlatformAgentRuntime or ExecutionGateway, turn a conclusion into
execution, fabricate citations, or hide conflicting evidence.

Permissions: RESEARCH_READ (viewer), RESEARCH_CREATE/EDIT/CHALLENGE (operator+),
RESEARCH_REVIEW/PUBLISH (owner+). Human publication approval is server-owned.
