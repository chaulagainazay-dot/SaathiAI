# M60 — Workflow Templates

Routes: `/platform/templates` (catalog), `/platform/workflows` (launcher),
`/platform/workflows/new`, `/platform/workflows/[workflowId]` (detail). Behavior:
**LOCAL_WORKFLOW_TEMPLATE**. Five templates (operational review, research task,
documentation audit, release readiness, incident investigation) each with objective,
inputs, stages, roles, tools, approval points, evidence, risk. Starting a workflow
prefills a LOCAL mission draft and routes to governed mission creation. Templates
grant no authority, hold no secrets, do not execute, and do not bypass approvals.
