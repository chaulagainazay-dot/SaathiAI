**DAY 4 — Quick Win Post (Instant value)**

**Post Template 4:**
```
Writing Task 1 मा एउटा mistake जसले तिम्रो band घटाउँछ 📉

"The graph shows an increase."

WRONG — यो basic छ।

BETTER:

"The graph illustrates a steady/sharp/gradual increase of X%..."

Examiner adjectives खोज्छ। "increase" alone = Band 5.

Try गर — यी 3 sentences लेख:

1. एउटा line graph describe गर — "steadily rose by..."
2. एउटा bar chart — "significantly higher than..."
3. एउटा pie chart — "accounted for nearly a third of..."
```
