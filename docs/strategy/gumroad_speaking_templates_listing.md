- What examiners want from Part 3 (abstract thinking, opinions with reasons)

  - **Speculating**: "It's hard to say for certain, but I'd imagine...", "I suppose it depends on..."
  - **Comparing**: "In contrast to...", "Whereas X tends to...", "There's a significant difference between..."
  - **Conceding and pivoting**: "I can see where that argument comes from, but..."
