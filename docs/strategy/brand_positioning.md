# Brand Positioning
## pielts / Mr. Yeti IELTS Brand

---

## The "Only I" Statement

**pielts is the only IELTS preparation brand that combines a free AI-powered practice platform with daily short-form video lessons — delivered by a relatable cartoon character who speaks to South Asian students from a place of shared experience, not corporate distance.**

No other brand in the IELTS space does all three simultaneously:
1. Free, real-time practice tests (not just sample PDFs)
2. Daily video content (not weekly or sporadic)
3. A persona rooted in South Asian student identity

---

| Brand | Strength | Weakness vs. pielts |

---

## Key Differentiators

### 1. AI-First Production at Zero Marginal Cost
Mr. Yeti's videos are produced using AI video generation (Veo AI), allowing daily publishing on a $90/month budget. This is not a weakness — it is the scalability moat. No competitor producing human-fronted content can match daily output at this cost.

### 2. Free Practice Platform (pielts.web.app)
The video content is not the product — it is the top of the funnel. The platform is the product. Students watch Mr. Yeti, then go practice. No competitor combines viral short-form content with an integrated practice platform.

### 3. Mr. Yeti — The Character Advantage
Mr. Yeti is not a generic teacher. He is a cartoon Yeti in a teacher suit and glasses — friendly, non-threatening, and visually memorable. In a feed full of talking-head IELTS teachers, a Yeti stands out immediately. The character also travels across cultures without requiring a single human face.

### 4. South Asian Student Empathy
The content is made by someone who understands why Band 6.5 matters for a Nepali nurse or why Band 7.5 is life-changing for an Indian IT professional. The tone, examples, and pain points come from lived proximity — not market research.

### 5. Daily Consistency
Most IELTS content brands post 1–4 times per week. Mr. Yeti posts every day. For students in a 3-month study window, daily content creates habit and dependency.

---

## Positioning Statement (Full)

*For IELTS aspirants in South and Southeast Asia who need effective preparation without the cost of coaching centers, pielts is the free AI-powered IELTS platform that delivers daily video lessons through Mr. Yeti and real practice tests on pielts.web.app — unlike premium courses that charge $100+ or coaching centers that are geographically out of reach, pielts makes Band 6.5+ achievable for anyone with a smartphone.*
