### Group Rules (5 Rules)

1. **No spam or self-promotion.** No links to other IELTS courses, coaching centres, or external Facebook groups. First offence = warning, second = removal.
2. **Keep it IELTS-relevant.** Posts must relate to IELTS preparation, results, study strategies, or language learning. Off-topic posts will be removed without warning.
3. **Be constructive, not cruel.** When giving feedback on Writing or Speaking samples, be kind. Praise what works. Suggest, don't criticise.
4. **Share your score journey.** We encourage members to post their results — band 5 or band 8, all scores are welcome. This is a judgment-free learning zone.
5. **Verify before you claim.** Don't share exam "leaks", rumour-based tips, or unverified band score shortcuts. If you're not sure, ask — don't assume.

## 2. Engagement Strategy

### How to Respond to Comments on Videos

**Template 1: Appreciation comment ("Love this tip!", "Thank you!")**
> So glad it helped! 😊 If you want more like this, try the full practice on pielts.web.app — it's free and there's a lot more where that came from. What section are you working on next?

**Template 2: Question comment ("What about Writing Task 1?")**
> Great question! Task 1 is actually something Mr. Yeti covers a lot — [link to specific video or: "check the pinned playlist"]. Short answer: [give a 1-sentence tip]. Want a full breakdown? Drop "Task 1" in the comments and I'll pin a guide.

**Template 3: Struggle comment ("I keep failing Listening")**
> Don't give up — most students feel this way before they crack it. The #1 fix: [specific tip]. Also try the Listening practice on pielts.web.app — it's free and gives you instant feedback. You've got this. 🏔️

**Template 4: Score share ("I got 6.0 but need 6.5")**
> 6.0 is a great base to build from! The gap to 6.5 is usually one or two specific fixes — not a total overhaul. What was your weakest section? Drop it below and I'll give you a focused plan.

**Template 5: Negative/sceptical comment ("These tips don't work")**
> Fair point — no single tip is a magic fix. IELTS is a system, not a trick. What specific problem are you running into? Tell me your situation and I'll give you something real to try. No fluff, I promise.

## 5. User Generated Content Campaign

### Campaign: "My IELTS Score Journey"

**Concept:** Invite students to share their before-and-after IELTS score story — where they started, what they changed, and where they landed. Repurpose as testimonials, social proof, and inspiration content.

---

### How to Run It

**Step 1 — Announce the campaign**
Post in the Facebook group + Stories on Instagram/TikTok:
> "We're collecting IELTS score stories. Share yours — Band 4 to Band 7, exam fail to exam pass, any journey counts. Best story gets [incentive]. Drop your story in the comments or DM us."

**Step 2 — Collect submissions**
Accept via:
- Facebook group post comment
- Instagram DM
- Google Form linked in bio (captures email for list)

**Step 3 — Feature the stories**
- Create a short-form video for each story (Mr. Yeti narrates their journey over a simple animation)
- Post to all 5 platforms with student's first name + country
- Tag the student so they share it to their own network

**Step 4 — Sustain it**
Run as an ongoing monthly feature. One student story per week = 52 pieces of authentic social proof per year.

---

### Incentive Options (Low/No Cost)

| Incentive | Cost | Value |
|----------|------|-------|
| Featured on Mr. Yeti's channel | Free | High (social proof for them) |
| 1-month "Band 7 Club" badge in group | Free | Community status |
| Free personalised Writing feedback from Mr. Yeti | 15 mins of time | High perceived value |
| Shoutout on all 5 platforms | Free | Reach boost for student |
| PDF personalised study plan | Free (template-based) | Practical value |

---

### How to Repurpose UGC

- **Short-form video:** Student quote over their score journey graphic → TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- **Testimonial graphic:** Pull best quote → Instagram carousel slide or Facebook post
- **Email social proof:** Use 2–3 stories in nurture emails to new subscribers
- **Website testimonials:** Add to pielts.web.app landing page
- **YouTube:** Compile 3–4 stories into a "Real Student IELTS Journeys" video monthly
