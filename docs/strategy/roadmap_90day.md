## Month 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)
**Theme:** Systems locked in. List building begins. Every video earns its keep.

### What's Already Done ✅
- Daily AI video pipeline live (Veo 3.1, $90/28-day budget)
- 5 platforms connected (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn)
- Brand Foundation docs written
- pielts.web.app live with free practice tests

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Lead Magnet + CTA Setup
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Create Band 7 Cheatsheet PDF | 1-page: Writing Task 2 templates, key vocabulary, band descriptors. Design in Canva. |
| Set up email platform | Use MailerLite (free up to 1,000 subs). Create welcome sequence placeholder. |

| Create landing page for cheatsheet | Simple page (can use MailerLite landing page builder — free) |

**Week 1 Target:** Cheatsheet live, email capture working, CTA added to top 10 performing videos.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Content Optimization
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|

| Standardize video end screen | Last 5 seconds: "Follow Mr. Yeti for daily IELTS tips + link in bio for free cheatsheet" |
| Post 1 Shorts/Reel per day | Repurpose existing long videos into 30–60s clips for Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts |
| Pin cheatsheet comment | Pin a comment with the cheatsheet link on every video |
| Set up basic analytics tracking | Track weekly: YouTube subs, email subs, pielts.web.app visitors |

**Week 2 Target:** 20 email subscribers, consistent Shorts/Reels posting.

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): Email Sequence Live
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Write + schedule 7-email welcome sequence | (Full copy in email_nurture_sequence.md) |
| Test sequence end-to-end | Subscribe yourself and verify all emails arrive correctly |

| Engage in 3 IELTS Facebook Groups | Comment helpfully (NOT spam). Reference Mr. Yeti / pielts.web.app where relevant. |

**Week 3 Target:** 50 email subscribers, email sequence fully live.

### Week 4 (Days 22–30): Review + Prepare Month 2
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|

- ✅ Email subscribers: 100

## Month 2 — Growth (Days 31–60)
**Theme:** Community + nurture + first product tease. Turn followers into warm leads.

### Week 5 (Days 31–37): Facebook Group Launch
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Launch "Mr. Yeti IELTS Community" Facebook Group | Private group. Set rules: no spam, English only, share your IELTS scores. |
| Welcome post from Mr. Yeti | "Hi! I'm Mr. Yeti. I post daily IELTS tips. This group is for students who want Band 7+. Introduce yourself below!" |
| Post daily in the group | 1 tip/question per day. Can repurpose video content. |
| Promote group in all video descriptions | "Join the FREE Mr. Yeti IELTS Community (link in bio)" |
| Send email to list about group | Email subject: "Come join our IELTS family 🎉" |

**Week 5 Target:** 100 Facebook Group members.

### Week 6 (Days 38–44): Value Deepening
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Launch weekly "Band 7 Challenge" in group | Students share one Task 2 essay. Mr. Yeti gives 1 tip on best submission. |
| YouTube: Go live once (or post Q&A video) | "Answer your IELTS questions" — builds real connection |
| Email: Send content emails (not just automated) | 1 broadcast email per week with a quick IELTS tip |
| Instagram: Add link-in-bio tool | Use Linktree or Beacons — link to cheatsheet, pielts.web.app, YouTube, Facebook Group |
| Collaborate ask #1 | DM 2 IELTS influencers: "Would you share my free Band 7 Cheatsheet with your audience in exchange for a shoutout?" |

**Week 6 Target:** 200 email subscribers, 250 Facebook Group members.

### Week 7 (Days 45–51): Product Tease
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|

| Start building premium product | Based on survey results — begin outlining (see product_ladder.md) |

**Week 7 Target:** 300 email subscribers, 500 Facebook Group members.

### Week 8 (Days 52–60): Nurture + Pre-Launch
| Action | Detail |

**Theme:** First revenue. Launch. Learn. Scale what works.

### Week 11 (Days 75–82): Webinar Monetization
| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|

**Week 11 Target:** $200–300 total revenue to date.

| Action | Detail |

**Month 3 Targets:**
- ✅ Revenue: $300–500/month
