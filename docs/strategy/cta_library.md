## MID CTAs — Engagement (10)
*Goal: comments, shares, community interaction — algorithm fuel*

---

**M1. Comment your target band score below**
- Platform: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Group, YouTube
- Position: Mid-video or end of video / caption
- Character count: 38
- Notes: High-conversion comment prompt. Add "👇 I'll reply to every one" to increase follow-through.

---

**M2. Tell me: Writing or Speaking — which scares you more?**
- Platform: TikTok, Instagram Stories (poll sticker), YouTube community tab
- Position: End of post or mid-video
- Character count: 56
- Notes: Binary choice = easy to answer = high engagement. Use the Instagram poll sticker for zero-friction engagement.

---

**M3. Drop your exam date in the comments — let's count down together**
- Platform: Facebook Group, YouTube, TikTok
- Position: End of video or pinned comment
- Character count: 63
- Notes: Creates community accountability. Follow up with a reply containing a study plan for their timeframe.

---

**M4. Share this with someone who keeps failing IELTS**
- Platform: Facebook, WhatsApp-targeted posts, Instagram
- Position: End of caption, end of video
- Character count: 50
- Notes: Emotion-driven share. "Keeps failing" hits harder than "preparing for." Use after a motivational or myth-bust video.

---

**M5. Comment "CHEATSHEET" and I'll send you the free PDF**
- Platform: TikTok (if DM is enabled), Instagram, Facebook
- Position: End of video / caption
- Character count: 54
- Notes: Keyword comment CTA. Drives comments (algorithm boost) and captures interested leads. Requires a process to DM responders manually or via automation.

---

**M6. Which tip surprised you most? Number it in the comments**
- Platform: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
- Position: End of carousel or list-format video
- Character count: 55
- Notes: Works after "5 tips" or "7 mistakes" content. Forces the viewer to process and recall, which increases retention time.

---

**M7. Tag a friend who needs to hear this before their exam**
- Platform: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
- Position: End of post / end of video
- Character count: 53
- Notes: Organic reach multiplier. Works best after emotional or surprising content — not for dry tips.

---

**M8. Repost this to your story — your IELTS friends need it**
- Platform: Instagram Stories
- Position: Final story frame
- Character count: 54
- Notes: Direct instruction increases repost rate. Pair with a "swipe up" or a sticker to make it easy.

---

**M9. What's the hardest IELTS question you've ever seen? Drop it below**
- Platform: YouTube, Facebook Group, TikTok
- Position: Pinned comment or end of video
- Character count: 65
- Notes: Open-ended question generates long comments. Reply with a quick tip to model good community behaviour.

---

**M10. Try this today and tell me how it went in the comments**
- Platform: TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Group
- Position: End of "how-to" or strategy videos
- Character count: 54
- Notes: Action assignment increases video completion and return comments. Works well with Writing or Speaking technique videos.
