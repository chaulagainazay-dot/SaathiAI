# Brand Voice & Tone Guide
## Mr. Yeti / pielts

---

## Voice Character Overview

Mr. Yeti is a cartoon Yeti in a teacher suit and glasses. He is warm, slightly nerdy, and deeply invested in his students passing IELTS. He has the patience of a tutor and the enthusiasm of someone who genuinely loves language learning.

He is NOT:
- A corporate brand account
- A news anchor reading from a script
- A hype machine using motivational-poster language
- A Western IELTS teacher who doesn't understand what Band 6.5 means to a South Asian family

He IS:
- Your senior study buddy who aced IELTS and wants to help you do the same
- Direct about what works and what wastes your study time
- Honest about the difficulty without being discouraging
- Culturally fluent in South Asian student anxieties

## Do / Don't Examples

### DO: Be specific and actionable

> **DO:** "For IELTS Task 2, write your thesis in the first paragraph — not in the conclusion. Band 7 essays always state the position upfront."
>
> **DON'T:** "Writing a great IELTS essay takes practice and dedication. Keep working hard!"

---

### DO: Acknowledge the real stakes

> **DO:** "If you need Band 6.5 for your UK visa, here's what the examiner is actually counting in your speaking."
>
> **DON'T:** "IELTS is a great opportunity to show your English skills!"

---

### DO: Use simple, direct language

> **DO:** "Collocations. Learn them. Use them. They are the fastest way to raise your vocabulary score."
>
> **DON'T:** "Incorporating sophisticated lexical collocations into your written discourse may facilitate a more favorable band score assessment."

---

### DO: Be encouraging without being hollow

> **DO:** "You failed Band 6? That means you know the exam now. Most people improve 0.5–1 band on the second attempt. Use that knowledge."
>
> **DON'T:** "Believe in yourself! You can do it! IELTS is just a test!"

---

### DON'T: Use corporate filler

Avoid: "We're passionate about...", "World-class...", "Innovative solutions...", "Empowering learners...", "Holistic approach..."

## Vocabulary Guide

### Use These Words
- Practice (not "study session")
- Band score / Band 6.5 / Band 7 (always specific)
- Examiner (not "teacher" when talking about test)
- Tip / trick / shortcut (for video hooks)
- Free (use it — it's the brand's biggest selling point)
- Try this / do this / avoid this (imperative, active)
- Today's tip / Quick tip / One thing (for short-form hooks)

### Avoid These Words
- World-class
- Premium experience (use "premium features" only when describing the paid tier)
- Journey (overused in education)
- Synergy, leverage, holistic
- "Guys" (use "everyone" or just address them directly)

**Sample Short caption:**
```

Here's what to do instead 👇

✅ Predict the answer TYPE before the audio plays
✅ Listen for synonyms, not exact words

Practice free on pielts.web.app 🎯

#IELTS #IELTSTips #IELTSListening #Band7
```

### TikTok

**Tone:** Fastest and most hook-driven. The first 1.5 seconds must earn the scroll-stop. Short sentences. Line breaks after every thought. Direct address.

**Sample caption:**
```
IELTS Writing Task 1 mistake everyone makes ❌

You: "The graph shows..."
Examiner: boring.

Try this instead:

"Overall, X increased significantly while Y remained stable."

Put the overview in paragraph 1.
Not the conclusion.

Band 6.5 → Band 7 in one change.

Practice free: pielts.web.app

#IELTS #IELTSTips #Band7 #StudyTok #IELTSWriting
```

### Instagram (Reels + Feed)
