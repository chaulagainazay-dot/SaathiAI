## 6. When to Pivot or Accelerate

### Pivot signals (if these occur by Month 3)
- Average video views consistently under 200 across all platforms → reconsider content format, hook structure, or posting time
- pielts.web.app bounce rate above 80% → UX or page speed issue needs fixing
- Zero premium conversions after 200 registered free users → pricing or paywall positioning needs review

### Acceleration signals (if these occur)
- Any video reaches 100K views → repost it on all platforms, make a follow-up series
- Organic search traffic growing 20%+ month-over-month → invest in more SEO-optimized blog posts to compound it
- WhatsApp/Facebook group self-organizes → nurture it as a community moat, not just a traffic channel
