# Brand Vision, Mission & Core Purpose
## pielts / Mr. Yeti IELTS Brand

---

## Vision

**By 2030, pielts is the #1 free IELTS preparation platform for South and Southeast Asian students — with 500,000 monthly active users, a recognized Mr. Yeti content brand across YouTube and TikTok, and a sustainable subscription revenue stream that funds Ajay's relocation abroad and ongoing product development.**

Specifically, in 5 years:
- pielts.web.app serves students in 15+ countries with full AI-powered mock test capabilities
- Mr. Yeti has 500K+ YouTube subscribers and 200K+ TikTok followers
- Premium tier generates $5,000+ MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- The brand is a recognized alternative to expensive paid IELTS coaching in Nepal, India, Bangladesh, and Philippines
- A library of 1,000+ short-form IELTS tips videos exists as a permanent free resource

---

## Mission

**pielts publishes one IELTS tip daily — through Mr. Yeti — so that any South Asian student with a smartphone can study for free, practice on pielts.web.app, and walk into their IELTS exam with real confidence.**

Daily execution of this mission means:
- One short-form video (60-90 seconds) goes live on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook every day
- pielts.web.app maintains free IELTS practice tests for Reading, Writing, Listening, and Speaking
- Every piece of content points students toward a clear next step: practice on the platform
- Premium features are added steadily to reward students who want more depth

---

## Core Purpose

**IELTS coaching should not be a privilege.**

In Nepal, India, Bangladesh, and the Philippines, IELTS is not just a test — it is a gateway. A Band 6.5 means a UK nursing license. A Band 7.5 means a Canadian PR application. A Band 7 means an Australian university acceptance.

But the coaching industry has built a wall around this gateway. Coaching centers charge NPR 15,000–30,000 (≈ $110–$225 USD). Families in Pokhara, Dhaka, and Cebu pay these fees even when they cannot afford to. Students who cannot pay study alone with pirated PDFs.

pielts exists to tear down that wall. The brand is built on a belief that **free access to quality IELTS preparation is a social good** — and that AI-generated content, distributed at scale, can make that possible without venture capital or a large team.

Every video Mr. Yeti publishes, every free test on pielts.web.app, is a direct act against that inequality.
