# SaathiOS Product Speech Recording — Consent (v1)

**Consent version:** `saathi-product-speech-consent-v1`

You are invited to record short speech prompts for SaathiOS speech recognition improvement.

## What we collect

- Voice recordings of prompts shown to you
- Pseudonymous speaker ID (e.g. `spk_007`)
- Optional device label (Mac / phone / headset)
- Optional high-level region group (voluntary)

We do **not** need your legal name, government ID, home address, account numbers, or health information.

## How recordings may be used (you choose)

| Permission | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation | Measure recognition quality |
| Internal research | Internal experiments only |
| **Commercial model training** | Train SaathiOS product speech models |
| Redistribution | Share raw audio outside SaathiOS (default **no**) |

Commercial training requires an explicit **yes**.

## Storage

Recordings stay on the collector device under:

```text
~/.saathi/stt-product-corpus/
```

They are **not** uploaded automatically and **not** committed to public Git.

## Withdrawal

Contact the project operator with your `speaker_id` and recording date.  
Reference: `SPEAKER_CONSENT_SPEC.md` / operator process.

## Statement

I understand the above and choose my permissions voluntarily.
